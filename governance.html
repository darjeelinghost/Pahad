HOW PAHAD IS GOVERNED

Accountable leadership.
Democratic participation.

PAHAD’s governance model brings together democratic membership authority, collective oversight and professional executive leadership.

Governance
PAHAD · 01

General Assembly

The General Assembly is the broadest expression of membership authority. Its powers and procedures are defined by the Constitution and registered Rules and Regulations.

  • Receives institutional and financial reports
  • Elects the Governing Body through the prescribed process
  • Considers constitutional amendments and major institutional matters
PAHAD · 02

Governing Body

The seven-member Governing Body provides governance, oversight and strategic direction.

  • President
  • Vice President
  • General Secretary
  • Joint Secretary
  • Treasurer
  • Executive Member One
  • Executive Member Two
PAHAD · 03

Executive leadership

The Chief Executive Director leads implementation and coordinates the six Directorates. The Central Secretariat supports executive administration and coordination.

  • Chief Executive Director
  • Executive Secretary and Central Secretariat
  • Six functional Directorates
PAHAD · 04

Institutional safeguards

No office-bearer or executive leader has unrestricted personal authority. Decisions, financial responsibilities, elections and amendments must follow the governing documents.

  • Collective decision-making
  • Recorded resolutions
  • Financial accountability
  • Fair procedures and member rights
PAHAD · 05

Organisational structure

The six Directorates are functional areas of institutional organisation. Departments and units may be activated progressively as capacity develops.

  • Research, Knowledge & Innovation
  • Civic & Community Development
  • Economic Development & Enterprise
  • Media, Communications & Culture
  • Policy, Governance & Public Affairs
  • Administration, Finance & Institutional Development
