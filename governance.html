GOVERNANCE

What you need to know

Explore the information below and follow the links for the next step.

01

Governing body

The Governing Body provides overall institutional oversight, safeguards the purpose and principles of PAHAD, and considers major organisational matters.

02

Executive administration

Executive responsibilities coordinate approved plans, day-to-day administration, communication and implementation.

03

Six directorates

The institutional working framework includes Research, Knowledge and Innovation; Civic and Community Development; Economic Development and Enterprise; Media, Communications and Cultural Documentation; Policy, Governance and Public Affairs; and Administration, Finance and Institutional Development.

04

Progressive development

The proposed departments under the directorates are an organisational design to activate progressively. They do not imply that every role or department is currently staffed.

05

Accountability

Decisions, records, finances and programme activities should be documented and reviewed in accordance with adopted rules and policies.
