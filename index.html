PAHAD brings people, knowledge and collective action together to support inclusive and sustainable development across the Darjeeling and Kalimpong hills and adjoining areas.

PAHAD · 01

Our purpose

We create space for young people, professionals, students, researchers, entrepreneurs and community members to identify local priorities, contribute ideas and take part in practical solutions.

  • Community-led development
  • Youth leadership and participation
  • Research, learning and innovation
PAHAD · 02

Five areas of work

Our work connects people’s needs with practical action across five complementary areas.

  • Human Development
  • Livelihood Development
  • Community Development
  • Research and Innovation
  • Himalayan Sustainability and Heritage
PAHAD · 03

How we are organised

PAHAD combines democratic membership governance, accountable oversight and professional executive leadership. Its six Directorates support coordinated institutional work.

  • Research, Knowledge & Innovation
  • Civic & Community Development
  • Economic Development & Enterprise
  • Media, Communications & Culture
  • Policy, Governance & Public Affairs
  • Administration, Finance & Institutional Development
