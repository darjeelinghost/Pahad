WHO WE ARE

A place to belong.
A purpose to build.

PAHAD is a platform for people who believe that stronger communities begin with informed, capable and united people.

We bring people together to learn, exchange ideas, develop skills and take part in meaningful community initiatives. We believe in dignity, participation, knowledge and collective responsibility.

Our aim is to create an environment where young people and communities can recognise their strengths and work together towards a better future.
