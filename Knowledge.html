KNOWLEDGE
BEYOND DOCUMENTS

Knowledge lives
in people too.

Knowledge is not limited to academic research or published documents. It also exists in people's experiences, professional skills, local practices, community histories and everyday lives.

PAHAD seeks to recognise, collect, organise and connect these different forms of knowledge.

CONTRIBUTE KNOWLEDGE

Share what you know.
Help others learn.

Have a research paper, report, publication, educational resource, documentation or other useful material to share? We welcome relevant contributions that can help preserve knowledge, improve understanding and support community development.
