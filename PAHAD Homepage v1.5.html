Install PAHAD Web App

Add PAHAD to your home screen or desktop for a fast, full-screen app experience:

  1. iPhone / iPad (Safari): Tap the Share button (⍐) at the bottom of the screen, then select Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android (Chrome): Tap the browser menu (⋮) and select Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Desktop (Chrome / Edge): Click the Install PAHAD icon in the address bar.

WHO WE ARE

A shared purpose.
A collective future.

PAHAD brings people, knowledge and institutions together to help communities identify priorities, participate in solutions and shape a more sustainable future.

We believe meaningful development grows through participation, cooperation, research, innovation and respect for human dignity. Our work is guided by an inclusive approach that welcomes people with different experiences and viewpoints.
