WHAT WE DO

Five connected areas.
One shared purpose.

PAHAD’s five Areas of Work describe the development outcomes it pursues. Its six Directorates describe how the institution organises its work.

Our Work
PAHAD · 01

01 · Human Development

Learning, skills, leadership and personal growth, especially for young people.

  • Education and learning
  • Skills and capacity building
  • Youth leadership
PAHAD · 02

02 · Livelihood Development

Livelihoods, entrepreneurship and local economic opportunity.

  • Enterprise development
  • Livelihood skills
  • Local economic opportunity
PAHAD · 03

03 · Community Development

Local priorities, civic participation and community-led action.

  • Community initiatives
  • Participation and inclusion
  • Local problem-solving
PAHAD · 04

04 · Research and Innovation

Evidence, documentation, data and practical innovation.

  • Research and documentation
  • Knowledge-sharing
  • Appropriate innovation
PAHAD · 05

05 · Himalayan Sustainability and Heritage

Environmental sustainability, culture and heritage across the Himalayan region.

  • Environmental awareness
  • Cultural documentation
  • Heritage and sustainability
PAHAD · 06

How the work is organised

Six Directorates provide expertise and coordination. Himalayan Sustainability and Heritage is cross-directorate, not assigned to a single Directorate.

  • Research, Knowledge & Innovation
  • Civic & Community Development
  • Economic Development & Enterprise
  • Media, Communications & Culture
  • Policy, Governance & Public Affairs
  • Administration, Finance & Institutional Development
