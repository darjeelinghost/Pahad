PAHAD welcomes people who want to learn, contribute, collaborate and take part in community development.

Membership
PAHAD · 01

Membership categories

PAHAD recognises six membership categories. Eligibility, rights, contributions and procedures are governed by the Constitution and Rules and Regulations.

  • Founder Member
  • Life Member
  • Ordinary Member
  • Associate Member
  • Student Member
  • Honorary Member
PAHAD · 02

Ways to contribute

Members may express their interests and skills and explore suitable opportunities as the institution develops.

  • Institutional leadership and coordination
  • Research and documentation
  • Programme and project support
  • Communications and cultural documentation
  • Technical and professional advice
  • Volunteering and general participation
PAHAD · 03

What membership means

Membership provides a way to participate in PAHAD. It does not automatically guarantee employment, grants, scholarships, funding, elected office or a place in a particular programme.
