Who may apply

People who support PAHAD’s purpose and principles may express their interest through the official membership form.

02

Membership categories

The applicable categories, eligibility and rights should be read from the current official membership form and adopted institutional documents.

03

Application process

Submit the membership form with accurate information. The institution may contact applicants if clarification or verification is required.

04

Members may contribute ideas, knowledge, time, skills and participation in accordance with the rules and opportunities available.

05

Personal information submitted through the membership form should be handled in accordance with the published privacy notice and applicable requirements.
