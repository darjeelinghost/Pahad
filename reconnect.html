We may have moved in different directions. But what we built together still matters.

From DSSCOLONY to PAHAD

The name has changed.
The spirit has not.

DSSCOLONY was more than a name. It was a spirit of togetherness: our willingness to begin, experiment, collaborate and believe that ordinary people could create something meaningful for their community.

Those years taught us that enthusiasm can bring people together, but a lasting institution needs a stronger foundation, responsible leadership and a purpose that can continue beyond any one person.

That is why we are beginning a new chapter through PAHAD — A Holistic Development Institution. Our work now looks towards education, research, skill development, livelihoods, culture, community institutions, enterprise and opportunities for people to realise their potential.

“PAHAD is not asking you to forget DSSCOLONY. It is inviting you to be part of what DSSCOLONY helped inspire.”
There is room for you

You don't have to
start where you left off.

You do not need to explain why you stepped away. You do not need to be the same person you were in 2018. We have all grown, learned different things and followed different paths.

Whether you were a founding member, volunteer, creative contributor, event participant, collaborator or someone who simply believed in the idea, we would love to reconnect.

Bring your experience, your skills, your ideas—or simply your willingness to be part of something meaningful again. There is no expectation that everyone must contribute in the same way.

We are not trying to recreate 2018. We are inviting you to help build what comes next.

An open invitation to every former member

The door is open.
Come, let's begin again.

Let us reconnect, rediscover our collective strength and build something more enduring together. The friendships, the courage and the shared purpose that brought us together still have a place in this journey.

The best part of our journey may still be ahead of us.
