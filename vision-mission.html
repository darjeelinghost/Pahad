WHAT WE MUST NEVER OVERLOOK

recognising people.

No valuable contribution should go unnoticed. No useful knowledge should go unused. No one should be held back simply because they lack the right connections or opportunities.

Local strength.
Regional possibility.

Our work is rooted in the lived realities, identities and aspirations of Darjeeling and Kalimpong, the adjoining Terai and Dooars, Sikkim and the wider Himalayan region.

We believe regional progress grows when local people are heard, local knowledge is valued, local resources are used wisely and institutions work together.
