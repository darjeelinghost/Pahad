OPENNESS AND ACCOUNTABILITY

Trust is built
through accountability.

PAHAD aims to make its institutional decisions, responsibilities and use of resources understandable and appropriately accessible.

Transparency
PAHAD · 01

Institutional accountability

PAHAD should maintain accurate records and make relevant institutional information available in accordance with its governing documents, privacy obligations and applicable law.

  • Annual institutional reports
  • Approved financial statements and audit information
  • Governance and meeting records, where disclosable
  • Programme summaries and outcomes
PAHAD · 02

Financial responsibility

All receipts and expenditure should be documented. Institutional funds and property are held for PAHAD’s approved objectives and must not be used for personal benefit.

PAHAD · 03

Policies and disclosures

Publish only current, approved documents. Each document should show its title, version, approval date and status.

  • Privacy and Data-Handling Policy
  • Finance and Procurement Policy
  • Conflict of Interest Policy
  • Safeguarding and Complaints Procedure
  • Annual reports and approved disclosures
PAHAD · 04

A note on availability

Do not imply that an audit, policy or report exists before it has been completed and approved.
