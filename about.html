WHO WE ARE

A community institution
rooted in the Himalayas.

PAHAD — Participatory Action for Holistic Advancement & Development — is an independent, non-partisan, non-profit community development institution.

About PAHAD
PAHAD · 01

Our identity

PAHAD works to create more opportunities for people and communities in the Darjeeling and Kalimpong hills and adjoining areas.

  • Independent and non-partisan
  • Community-centred
  • Knowledge-informed
  • Open to collaboration
PAHAD · 02

Our approach

We believe lasting progress grows when people understand the issues around them, take part in decisions and work together on practical responses.

  • Listen to communities
  • Understand needs through evidence
  • Build partnerships and local capacity
  • Act, learn and improve
PAHAD · 03

Our guiding idea

People, Knowledge, Action. These three elements connect community participation, informed thinking and practical implementation.
